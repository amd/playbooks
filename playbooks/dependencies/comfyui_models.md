### ComfyUI Models

<!-- @device:halo_box -->
> **Note:** If the models are already available in ComfyUI, you can skip this section. Otherwise, follow the steps below to download them.
<!-- @device:end -->

If you previously deleted pre-downloaded ComfyUI models or want to download those for the first time, simply press the `Download` button on each of the models when prompted that missing models have been detected.
You may need to manually place the model files in the correct locations. 

<!-- @device:halo_box -->
<!-- @os:linux -->
> **Note:** On the AMD Ryzen™ AI Halo, ComfyUI runs in a prebuilt container. Place any manually downloaded model files under `~/.local/share/ComfyUI/models` (in the matching `diffusion_models`, `text_encoders`, or `vae` subfolder) so the containerized ComfyUI can find them.
<!-- @os:end -->
<!-- @device:end -->
